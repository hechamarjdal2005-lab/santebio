A Moroccan laboratory built on trust

Santé Bio is a natural health and cosmetics laboratory founded in Morocco. We formulate, manufacture and certify cosmetic products to international standards, then distribute them through the pharmacy network that families already trust.

ISO 22716Certified since day one
Our story

We didn't want a shop. We wanted a standard.

Santé Bio started with a simple frustration: too many cosmetic products on Moroccan shelves made beautiful promises on the front and skipped the science on the back. So we built a laboratory instead of a storefront.

Today we develop shower gels, shampoos, vitamin packs, facial oils, vaseline, a retinol & collagen anti-aging line, anti-lice treatments and cold-pressed natural oils — each one formulated, tested and released under ISO 22716, the international cosmetic Good Manufacturing Practice standard.

Our products reach you the way they should: through pharmacies and parapharmacies in every region of Morocco, where a qualified advisor can guide you. And when you'd rather not travel, you order the same products from this site and we deliver to your door.

Our values

Four things we never compromise on

Every formulation decision comes back to these four words. They're printed on our lab wall, and they decide what makes it into your bathroom cabinet.

01

Certification

Our factory is audited against ISO 22716. Each batch carries a traceability record, and we release nothing that hasn't passed stability and safety testing.

02

Naturalness

Botanical extracts, cold-pressed oils and actives dosed at levels that actually work. If an ingredient doesn't earn its place, it doesn't go in the formula.

03

Accessibility

Certified quality shouldn't be a luxury. We keep prices honest and put our products in pharmacies across the country, not in one flagship store.

04

Trust

We'd rather earn a recommendation than buy an advert. Our community of 75,000+ followers tells us the truth, and we listen to every comment.

Our journey

Built step by step, region by region

Twelve years of building a Moroccan natural health brand, one formulation and one pharmacy at a time.

Where to find us
2014

The laboratory opens

A small formulation unit in Casablanca, with one obsession: make a shower gel that a pharmacist would recommend without hesitating.

2017

ISO 22716 certification

Our facility passes the international cosmetic GMP audit. Every batch becomes traceable from raw material to finished product.

2020

Into the pharmacy network

Distribution begins across pharmacies and parapharmacies, from Casablanca to Agadir. The retinol & collagen line launches the same year.

2023

A community of 75,000+

Our social following becomes a real community: product advice, routines and honest reviews, shared between Moroccan families.

Today

Over 1,200 partner pharmacies

And direct WhatsApp ordering for anyone who'd rather have the range delivered to their door.
